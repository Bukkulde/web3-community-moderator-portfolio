# Web3 Community Moderation Sample

## Welcome Message

Welcome to our Web3 community! 👋

We're happy to have you here. Please introduce yourself, respect other members, and feel free to ask questions.

## Community Rules

- Be respectful to everyone.
- No spam or scams.
- No harassment or abusive language.
- Don't share suspicious links.
- Follow the community guidelines.
- Report suspicious activity to moderators.

## Example Moderation Response

**Member:** "Someone is sending suspicious links in the chat."

**Moderator:** "Thanks for reporting this. Please don't click the link. I'll review the account and take the appropriate action."

## Community Engagement

I would encourage members to participate through discussions, questions, polls, and educational Web3 content.

## My Focus

- Community safety
- Member engagement
- Fast and helpful responses
- Keeping discussions organized
- Supporting a positive community environment
