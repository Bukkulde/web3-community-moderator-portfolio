# Web3 Community Engagement Sample

## Engagement Strategy

My goal is to keep the community active, welcoming, and informed.

### Daily Engagement Ideas

- Ask members a simple Web3 question.
- Share useful educational content.
- Welcome new members.
- Start discussions about crypto and Web3.
- Run polls and community activities.
- Respond to questions and feedback.

## Example Engagement Post

**GM Web3 community! 👋**

What's one Web3 project you're currently learning about?

Drop the project below and let's learn together. 🚀

## Member Support

I aim to respond to community questions clearly and respectfully while helping members find the right information.

## Goal

Build an active community where members feel welcomed, informed, and comfortable participating.
